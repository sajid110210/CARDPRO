Free Commercial Printing Suite · ID Cards, Passport Photos & AI Cutouts

Welcome to iLovePrint.in, a free utility platform crafted for CSC centers, digital service shops, cyber cafes, and professional photo studios across India. Our tools are calibrated at true 300 DPI (Dots Per Inch) output to ensure that digital files print with crisp lines, sharp barcodes, and accurate colors on all desktop inkjets, laser printers, and PVC card machines.

1. Standard ID Card Printing Specifications

Creating customer identity cards from government PDFs often involves uneven aspect ratios and blurry photos. Smart ID Studio Pro eliminates resizing guesswork with standard dimensions:

  • Open Lamination Pouch (17.0 × 5.5 cm): Standard front and back side joined horizontally with an optional center dotted fold line. Fits standard thermal pouches (70 × 100 mm / 65 × 95 mm).
  • CR-80 PVC Size (8.5 × 5.4 cm): Standard ISO plastic card dimensions used by thermal card printers (Evolis, Zebra, Magicard) and dragon sheet tray printing on Epson L805 / L850.
  • Selective Facial Enhancement: Dark document photos can be selectively brightened and sharpened without overexposing the surrounding text fields or QR codes.

2. Indian Passport Photo Sizing & Matrix Layout

Our Passport Size Photo Studio allows operators to load single or multiple customer portraits and automatically tile them across an A4 sheet (210 × 297 mm) with zero manual calculations:

  • Standard Dimensions: 3.0 × 3.5 cm or 3.5 × 4.5 cm with cutting border guides (1px to 3px width) to simplify scissor or guillotine trimming.
  • Auto-Fill Engine: Maximizes paper efficiency by calculating the exact row and column capacity of an A4 page, preventing wasted glossy photo paper.

3. Neural Background Matting & Crystal Clarity Upscaling

Customer photos taken against uneven shop walls or low lighting can be isolated in seconds using our integrated RMBG-1.4 Neural Matting Engine. All AI processing executes directly inside your web browser via WebAssembly, ensuring complete customer privacy since photos are never sent to external servers. Replace backgrounds with studio white, passport sky blue, or export transparent PNGs with 2×/4× resolution enhancements.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I paste files directly from the clipboard?

You can press Ctrl + V anywhere on the page after copying an image from WhatsApp Web, Photoshop, or a screenshot tool. The active studio will automatically detect the file and load it into your workspace.

How to set A4 printing page margins?

Our print stylesheet sets an exact 0.5 cm (5 mm) margin around the A4 page by default. You can adjust the Top, Bottom, Left, and Right margin fields directly in the print queue toolbar to match your printer's non-printable hardware margins.
